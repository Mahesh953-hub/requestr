### Summary

requestr's protocol parsers, PCAP writer, and Cronet engine already exist and are well tested, but every capture path collapses into one flat `captured_traffic` row with map-typed headers, a single `latencyMs`, fabricated timing splits, LIKE-based search, and an export layer that emits invalid HAR JSON. This plan normalizes persistence behind a single writer with WAL and three reviewable migrations, makes WebSocket/SSE/gRPC/trailers first-class, replaces every fabricated timing with a measured one, rewrites HAR/cURL/snippet export, closes the PCAP coverage hole, and wires Cronet as the API-Testr HTTP/3 sending client with real negotiated-protocol and downgrade evidence.

No existing codec is deleted. QUIC, QPACK, TLS, HPACK, protobuf, and the PCAP writer are all preserved and extended.
